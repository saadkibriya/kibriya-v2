Privacy-Preserving Vision-Based Detection of Pox Diseases Using Federated Learning

In Data-Driven Clinical Decision-Making Using Deep Learning in Imaging · Studies in Big Data, vol. 152 · Springer, Singapore · pp. 105–122 · 14 August 2024

Kibriya, M.G., Mistry, D., Mistry, D., Tonmoy, M.R., Ovi, S.H., Tabassum, A., Hossain, S. A federated learning framework for detecting pox-related skin diseases from visual data without centralising patient information — each node trains locally and only model weights are shared.

“Our framework achieves an impressive testing accuracy of 87.50% and undergoes a comprehensive evaluation, including key metrics such as Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, Loss curve, and Accuracy curve.”

Lightning Fatality Prediction over Bangladesh

Thesis research · BSc EEE, Pabna University of Science and Technology

Data-driven analysis of lightning strike fatality patterns across Bangladesh using meteorological records, geographic clustering and temporal modelling. The aim is a predictive system that can inform early-warning infrastructure.

Research Assistant · University of Asia Pacific · under Md. Shahidul Islam

Building and verifying a corpus of 1,178 Bangla sentences for downstream NLP tasks, including normalisation, quality-control pipelines and annotation protocols designed for low-resource Bengali data work. Separate from the chapter above — the sentence count belongs to this project.
