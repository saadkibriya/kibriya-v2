I didn’t start with a camera. I started with a need to remember things before they disappeared — golden hours over the Padma, fog over Bandarban at dawn, the silhouette of a stranger at a bus stop who looked like he was carrying the weight of a whole city.

2019Shooting as @_randomcitizen
6+Years of photographs
1.2KFollowers on Instagram

No studio, no DSLR, no assistant. Just me, whatever phone I had at the time, and the stubborn belief that the best light shows up when you aren’t ready for it. I’ve travelled across almost all of Bangladesh for these photographs — chasing sunsets in Cox’s Bazar, losing myself in the hills of Bandarban, shooting the chaos and quiet of Dhaka at 5 AM. This isn’t a portfolio. It’s a record of being somewhere and paying attention.
